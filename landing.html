FAQ

Honest answers.

If your question isn't here, email us — we read every one.

Does it really work offline?
Yes. EnterpriseCore is a desktop app (Electron). The backend (FastAPI + SQLite) runs on the same machine. The only thing that touches the internet is an outbound call to Claude or OpenAI if you choose to use a hosted AI provider — and you can disable that entirely and run Ollama locally instead.
What happens when I want to update?
You get updates included in your tier (1 year for Indie, 2 for Team, lifetime for Business). After that, your existing install keeps working forever — no remote kill switch, no license expiry. You can also renew updates for ~20% of your original price per additional year.
How does the AI work without sending my code anywhere?
Two modes. (1) BYO API key — you provide a Claude or OpenAI key, stored in your OS credential vault. Code only goes to your chosen provider, never to us. (2) Local mode — run Ollama on the same machine. Your code never leaves your hardware. Both work out of the box.
What about my existing data in Salesforce / QuickBooks / etc.?
We provide CSV/JSON importers for the major SaaS tools. For complex migrations, Team and Business tiers include 1–2 hours of migration assistance. Enterprise tier covers it end-to-end.
Multi-user from day one?
Yes. Switch the backend to Postgres (a config flag) and EnterpriseCore runs as a small on-prem server with role-based access for your whole team. Team tier supports 10 seats; Business supports 50.
What if I hate it?
30-day money-back guarantee. Email us within 30 days of purchase and we refund — no forms, no exit interviews.
